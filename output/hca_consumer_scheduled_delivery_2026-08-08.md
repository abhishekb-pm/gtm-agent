# Scheduled Delivery: Order Now, Get It When You Want

Hungry at noon but can't order until you're already starving? Scheduled Delivery lets you place your GrabFood order up to 24 hours in advance and choose the delivery window that suits you — whether that's tomorrow's office lunch or tonight's family dinner, sorted before the day gets busy.

---

## Who can use this, and where

**Right now, Scheduled Delivery is available in Singapore.** It's rolling out to Kuala Lumpur and other cities over the coming weeks.

In Singapore, you'll see the option if you've placed 3 or more GrabFood orders in the last 60 days. If you don't see it yet, check back soon — we're expanding access quickly.

> **Not seeing the "Schedule for later" option?** The restaurant you're browsing may not have opted in yet. Try a different restaurant, or order as usual and check again another day.

---

## How to place a scheduled order

1. Open GrabFood and choose a restaurant.
2. Below the delivery address bar, tap **"Schedule for later"**.
3. Pick a 30-minute delivery window from the next 24 hours. Greyed-out slots mean the restaurant is closed or delivery isn't available in your area at that time.
4. Add items to your cart and check out as normal. Your payment is charged at this step.
5. On the confirmation screen, you'll see your scheduled window confirmed — for example, *"Tomorrow, 12:30–1:00pm."*
6. Find your order anytime under **Orders → Upcoming Orders**, with a countdown to your delivery window.

That's it. We handle the rest — the restaurant gets notified at the right time, and your courier is dispatched so your food arrives within your chosen window.

---

## Reminders: what to expect and when

We'll send you two reminders before your order is prepared and dispatched:

| When | What we send |
|---|---|
| **60 minutes before your delivery window** | A push notification letting you know dispatch is coming up. This is your last chance to modify or cancel. |
| **15 minutes before your delivery window** | A heads-up that your order is about to be prepared. You can still cancel at this point (see cancellation rules below). |

**Important:** Reminders require push notifications to be turned on for the GrabFood app. If you have notifications off, your order still goes ahead as scheduled — we won't be able to hold it because you missed the alert.

To check: go to your phone's Settings → GrabFood → Notifications.

## Changing your order

### What you can change
- **Delivery window** — move to a different time slot (subject to availability)
- **Delivery address** — must be within our coverage area for your chosen window
- **Items** — add or remove items from your order

### What you can't change
- **The restaurant** — if you want to order from somewhere else, you'll need to cancel and place a new order.

### The cutoff: 60 minutes before your delivery window

**Once the 60-minute mark passes, modifications close.** You'll see a message in the app saying the modification window has closed, and no further changes can be made — by you or by our support team.

To make a change, go to **Orders → Upcoming Orders → [your order] → Modify order** before that cutoff.

---

## Cancelling your order

### Before the 60-minute cutoff: full refund, no questions asked

Cancel any time up to 60 minutes before your delivery window. Go to **Orders → Upcoming Orders → [your order] → Cancel order**.

**Refund timeline:** You'll receive a full refund to your original payment method within **3–5 business days**. If you paid with GrabPay credits, the refund is usually instant.

### After the 60-minute cutoff: standard cancellation policy applies

**If you try to cancel after the cutoff, the restaurant may have already started preparing your order.** At this point, the standard cancellation policy applies — this may mean a partial refund or no refund depending on preparation status.

If you believe there are exceptional circumstances, contact GrabFood support through the app. Note that our support team cannot override the cutoff as a general rule.

## If something goes wrong

We aim for every scheduled order to arrive on time. Here's what happens in the rare cases when things don't go to plan:

### The restaurant can't fulfil your order

Restaurants can flag that they're unable to fulfil a scheduled order up to 90 minutes before your delivery window. If this happens:

- We'll notify you immediately so you have time to order elsewhere.
- You'll receive a **full automatic refund** — 3–5 business days to your original payment method, or instantly if you paid with GrabPay credits.
- If the restaurant flags it very close to your window (after that 90-minute mark), you'll still get a full refund **plus an automatic courtesy voucher** for the inconvenience.

### No courier is available when your order dispatches

When it's time to dispatch your order, we'll spend up to 10 minutes finding you a courier. If we can't match one in time, we'll cancel the order automatically, notify you, and issue a **full refund** (3–5 business days, or instant for GrabPay credits).

This is uncommon — we forecast courier availability for scheduled windows in advance — but it can happen during unexpected surges.

### Your order is delayed

During very busy periods or unexpected platform issues, your delivery may arrive up to 15 minutes after your window closes. If this happens, we'll let you know in the app.

If your order arrives more than 30 minutes after your scheduled window, you're entitled to compensation under our standard delay policy. Contact support through the app.

### The restaurant closes unexpectedly after you've ordered

If a restaurant closes after your order is placed, our system detects this and cancels your order automatically. You'll get a notification and a **full refund** (3–5 business days, or instant for GrabPay credits).

## FAQs

**Can I place a scheduled order for less than 60 minutes from now?**
No. Scheduled Delivery is designed for orders placed at least 60 minutes before your chosen delivery window, so the restaurant and courier have time to prepare. For anything sooner, place a regular order.

---

**Can I schedule multiple orders at the same time — say, lunch and dinner?**
Yes. Each order is processed independently. You can have more than one scheduled order active at the same time, including from the same restaurant.

---

**Does Scheduled Delivery cost more than a regular order?**
The delivery fee for scheduled orders is the same as regular orders for your delivery address and distance. One benefit: if you schedule outside of peak hours (typically 12–1pm and 6–8pm), you may see lower surge pricing than you'd get ordering in the moment.

---

**What if I forget to pick up my food or I'm not home when it arrives?**
Once dispatched, your order follows the same process as any GrabFood delivery. If you're not reachable, your courier will follow the standard non-contact delivery or attempted delivery process. We're not able to hold or redirect a dispatched order.

---

**My delivery window has a 30-minute range. When exactly will my food arrive?**
Within that 30-minute window — we can't guarantee a specific minute. The window works the same way as any delivery estimate: traffic, preparation time, and distance all play a role. Plan for the end of the window if timing is critical.

---

**Can I use a promo code or GrabRewards on a scheduled order?**
Yes. Apply your promo code or rewards at checkout, exactly as you would for a regular order.

---

**I placed a scheduled order and now the app is crashing or I've been logged out. Is my order lost?**
No. Your order is saved on our servers, not just your phone. Log back in and go to **Orders → Upcoming Orders** — it'll be there.

---

**Can I change the restaurant after I've placed a scheduled order?**
You can't change the restaurant on an existing scheduled order. If you want a different restaurant, cancel your current order (before the 60-minute cutoff for a full refund) and place a new one.
